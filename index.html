NomCal

Log a meal by saying it out loud. A fast, ad-free calorie and macro tracker for iPhone.

  • Voice loggingSay "two eggs, toast with butter, and a coffee" and it becomes a logged meal with macros.
  • It learns your usual mealsOnce it knows your routine, "my usual breakfast plus an extra egg" is one sentence away from logged.
  • Barcode scanning and food searchPackaged foods from Open Food Facts, generic foods from the USDA database.
  • Recipes, weight tracking, and a home screen widgetBuild a recipe once, log a serving in a tap. Track weight and steps over time.
  • Apple Health integrationFree, never behind a paywall. Your health data never leaves your device.
